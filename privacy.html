Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Information you provide

Ate10 stores the account details you submit: name, username, email, password, and date of birth. You can also add a photo, a short bio, an Instagram handle, dish photographs, ratings, captions, comments, restaurant names, and food attributes. Feedback you send from the app is included.

Information generated through use

From ratings and attributes, Ate10 builds a Taste Profile and can calculate a Taste Match with someone else. Ate10 also sees which posts you like, who you follow, and the dishes you open.

How Ate10 uses information

Ate10 uses this information to run the app, show your posts, build Taste Profiles, calculate Taste Match, and keep the community within the guidelines. Ate10 may also use it to fix problems, understand how the app is used, send service messages, and develop features. Recommendations in the feed are not a separate paid system in this version.

On your device

Location on this device is used to suggest a nearby restaurant. Contacts stay on this device. Ate10 uses them only to match usernames and does not keep an address book. The camera and photo library are used to log a dish. A push token is stored on the account to send alerts about fires, follows, comments, and mentions.

Data sharing

Other people can see what you post, your username, your Instagram handle if you add one, your Taste Profile, and who you follow, according to what the app shows. Ate10 uses Supabase to store accounts and profiles. Place search can send a query to Apple’s map services. Push alerts are delivered through Apple’s push notification service.

Restaurants and partners

Ate10 does not currently sell personal information or share your account with restaurants.

Legal and safety

Ate10 may share information if the law requires it, or to investigate abuse, protect someone from harm, or respond to a valid legal request.

Business transfers

If Ate10 is sold, merged, or reorganized, account and content information may move with the product.

Data retention

Ate10 keeps account and post information while your account exists. Deleting the account from Settings starts a 30-day window. You can sign back in and restore it during that window. After 30 days, the login, profile, follows, and stored photos are removed.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from Settings. You can sign back in and restore it for 30 days. After that, the login, profile, follows, and photos stored with the account are removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses.

Security

Sign-in is handled by Ate10’s account provider.

Children

Ate10 is not for children under 13. Signup asks for a date of birth and blocks accounts under 13.

Changes to this policy

Ate10 can update this policy. The version and last-updated date will change. A material update can ask you to acknowledge it again.
