Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Starter legal copy. Review this before a public launch.

What this covers

This is a starter draft of how Ate handles information. It describes the product as it works today and a few uses Ate may add later. Where a practice is not built, it is marked for review.

Information you provide

Ate stores the account details you submit: name, username, email, password, and date of birth. You can also add a photo, a short bio, dish photographs, ratings, captions, comments, restaurant names, and food attributes. Feedback you send from the app is included.

Information generated through use

From ratings and attributes, Ate builds a Taste Profile and can calculate a Taste Match with someone else. Ate also sees which posts you like, who you follow, and the dishes you open. [Usage analytics — to be reviewed. This build does not describe a separate analytics product.]

How Ate uses information

Ate uses this information to run the app, show your posts, build Taste Profiles, calculate Taste Match, and keep the community within the guidelines. Ate may also use it to fix problems, understand how the app is used, send service messages, and develop features. Recommendations in the feed are not a separate paid system in this version.

Data sharing

Other people can see what you post, your username, your Taste Profile, and who you follow, according to what the app shows. Ate uses Supabase to store accounts and profiles. Place search can send a query to Apple’s map services. [Other service providers — to be listed.]

Restaurants and partners

Ate does not currently sell personal information or share your account with restaurants. Later, Ate may use aggregated or de-identified food, restaurant, rating, and taste data for research, insights, or commercial partnerships. This build does not produce a de-identified dataset, and this draft does not claim that it does. [Partner program — to be reviewed before it exists.]

Legal and safety

Ate may share information if the law requires it, or to investigate abuse, protect someone from harm, or respond to a valid legal request.

Business transfers

If Ate is sold, merged, or reorganized, account and content information may move with the product. [Notice for a transfer — to be added.]

Data retention

Ate keeps account and post information while your account exists. [Retention periods after deletion — to be added. Account deletion is not available in this version.]

Account deletion

You cannot delete your account from this version of the app. [Deletion request process — to be added.]

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses. [How to make a request — to be added.]

Security

Sign-in is handled by Ate’s account provider. [Security practices — to be reviewed. This draft does not describe encryption or a security certification.]

Children

Ate is not for children under 13. Signup asks for a date of birth and blocks accounts under 13. [What happens if a child account is discovered — to be added.]

Changes to this policy

Ate can update this policy. The version and last-updated date will change. A material update can ask you to acknowledge it again. That reminder is not built yet.
